Life Stages mod · texture review

Life Stages Textures

Every texture is drawn at 32×32 (HD) with modern hue-shifted shading: shadows lean cool, highlights lean warm, and outlines are a darker shade of the object’s own color. Characters use the standard skin layout at 128×128. Comment on any texture you want changed. A script builds all of them, so changes are quick.

NPC Moms
